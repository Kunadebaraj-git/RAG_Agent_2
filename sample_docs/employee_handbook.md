# Acme Corp Employee Handbook 2024

## Welcome

Welcome to Acme Corp! This handbook provides an overview of our company policies, benefits, and culture. Please read it carefully and refer to it whenever you have questions about company procedures.

## Company Mission

Acme Corp's mission is to deliver innovative solutions that transform how businesses operate. Founded in 2015, we have grown from a 5-person startup to a 500-employee company with offices in San Francisco, Austin, and London.

## Work Policies

### Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. We offer flexible scheduling with core hours between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM when all team members should be available. Remote work is permitted up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

### Paid Time Off (PTO)
- New employees: 15 days per year
- After 3 years: 20 days per year
- After 5 years: 25 days per year
- PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the HR portal

### Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A doctor's note is required for absences exceeding 3 consecutive days. Unused sick days do not carry over to the next year.

## Benefits

### Health Insurance
Acme Corp provides comprehensive health insurance through BlueCross BlueShield. The company covers 80% of premiums for employees and 60% for dependents. Plans include medical, dental, and vision coverage. Open enrollment occurs annually in November.

### 401(k) Retirement Plan
Employees are eligible for the 401(k) plan after 90 days of employment. The company matches 50% of contributions up to 6% of salary. Vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested after 4 years.

### Professional Development
Each employee receives a $2,000 annual learning budget for courses, conferences, and certifications. Additionally, the company provides access to LinkedIn Learning and internal mentorship programs.

## Code of Conduct

### Anti-Harassment Policy
Acme Corp maintains zero tolerance for harassment of any kind, including but not limited to sexual harassment, bullying, and discrimination based on race, gender, religion, age, disability, or sexual orientation. All complaints should be reported to HR immediately and will be investigated thoroughly.

### Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose proprietary information, trade secrets, or client data to unauthorized parties. All employees sign an NDA upon hiring. Violations may result in termination and legal action.

### Equipment
Company-provided equipment (laptop, monitor, peripherals) must be returned upon termination. Employees are responsible for reasonable care of equipment. Report any damage or theft to IT immediately.

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually in June and December. Reviews include self-assessment, manager assessment, and peer feedback. Compensation adjustments and promotions are considered during the December review cycle.
